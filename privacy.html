Familiekalender – privacy policy

Familiekalender is a private app used only by its owner's family.

  • It requests read-only access to Google Calendar and Google Tasks (calendar.readonly, tasks.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete anything.
  • The data is used only to display the family's calendars and tasks on a screen in the home.
  • Data and access tokens are kept on the family's own home server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
